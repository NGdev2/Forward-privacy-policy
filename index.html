Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-28  ·  Last updated: 2026-09-28

Forward is an offline RPG runner. There is no account and the game does not send your progress anywhere. The only data processing comes from the optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch. This policy explains it honestly.

1. Information We Collect

LOCAL Game progress and settings

Your hero's level, items, statistics and settings (language, volume, and similar) are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded to our servers — Forward has no servers. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

ADS Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Forward shows rewarded ads only when you tap a "Watch an ad" button (for example to double a reward, get back up after a defeat, or earn gems). To request and show these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system and app version)
  • Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched to the end) and diagnostics

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer. The developer only sees aggregated, anonymous reports (such as the number of ads shown).

No account, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer never receives your name, email or any content from your device.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To save your progress on your device so you can continue playing
  • To show the rewarded ads you choose to watch, measure them, and prevent ad fraud (by Google)
  • To give you the in-game reward after an ad is watched

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share any data with third parties other than Google AdMob as described here.

3. Your Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in a US state with applicable privacy laws, Forward shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse, or choose which purposes you allow.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy & ads. If you refuse, AdMob may still show limited, non-personalised ads where the law allows it.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

4. Third-Party Services

Forward uses the following third-party service, governed by Google's policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) — rewarded ads and the consent form. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

No other analytics SDKs, advertising networks or trackers are included in the app.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — needed to load ads. The rest of the game works offline.
  • Advertising ID — used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game progress stays on your device until you reset it (Settings → Reset progress) or uninstall the game. The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request or delete on our side. Data processed by Google for ads is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Forward is not directed at children under the age of 13, and its ads are configured for a teen audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's data has been processed through the app, please contact us.

8. Purchases

Forward currently has no in-app purchases. If they are added in the future, this policy will be updated before they are released.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the game evolves. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
